About Mindora

Mindora is an AI mental wellness companion created to give individuals a calm, non-judgmental space to explore their emotions, process daily stress, and feel heard.

What Mindora Is

  • A supportive, reflective conversational space for emotional exploration.
  • A companion to help untangle feelings of stress, sadness, loneliness, and overwhelm.
  • Equipped with an independent crisis safety layer to guide users to verified professional help.

What Mindora Is Not

  • Mindora is not a medical doctor, psychiatrist, or licensed clinical therapist.
  • Mindora cannot diagnose conditions or prescribe clinical treatments.
  • Mindora cannot provide emergency intervention directly during critical physical danger.

Privacy & Data Handling

Conversations are isolated to your account or guest session. We do not sell your personal reflections or conversation data. You can delete individual chats or your entire account at any time.
